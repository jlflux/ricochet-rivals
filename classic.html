HOUSE RULES

8-BALL

On a legal shot, hit one of your balls first. After contact, a ball must reach a rail or be pocketed. Sink one of your set to keep shooting.

The table stays open after the break until a legal solid or stripe is pocketed. Scratch or foul gives your opponent ball in hand.

After clearing your set, call a pocket for the 8. Sink it in that pocket without fouling to win. An early 8 or wrong pocket loses.
