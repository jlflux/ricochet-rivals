MATCH RULES

CLASSIC ZONE

1. Select one of your blue discs, drag backward to aim and set power, then release.
2. The AI takes a turn after you. Once both shots settle, the team with more discs inside the golden zone earns 1 point (ties earn no point).
3. Knock enemy discs into a corner pocket to eliminate them.
4. First to 5 points, or the last crew with a disc, wins.
